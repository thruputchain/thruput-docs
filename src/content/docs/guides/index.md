Work through a focused project using the [examples repository](https://github.com/thruputchain/thruput-examples):

- [Build a token](/guides/build-a-token/): balances, allowances, supply policy, events, and native/RV32 checks.
- [Build an AMM](/guides/build-an-amm/): pool creation, liquidity, queued swaps, and correlated outcomes, exercised through the contract test environments.
- [Consume the Firehose](/guides/consume-the-firehose/): connect to whole-block delivery, acknowledge durable progress, and resume after a disconnect.

For the full contract-to-application journey, follow the [counter tutorials](/tutorials/).
