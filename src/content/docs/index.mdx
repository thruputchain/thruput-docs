## Why Groundhog?

<div class="why-grid">
  <div class="why-item">
    <span class="why-header">
      <svg class="why-icon" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round"><path d="M13 2L3 14h9l-1 8 10-12h-9l1-8z"/></svg>
      <span class="why-title">Free Compute</span>
    </span>
    <span class="why-desc">No gas fees. Hold HOG tokens for capacity, not pay per operation. Storage is a refundable deposit, not a burn.</span>
  </div>
  <div class="why-item">
    <span class="why-header">
      <svg class="why-icon" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round"><polyline points="13 17 18 12 13 7"/><polyline points="6 17 11 12 6 7"/></svg>
      <span class="why-title">Parallel Execution</span>
    </span>
    <span class="why-desc">Typed storage primitives let independent and commutative transactions execute across available cores.</span>
  </div>
  <div class="why-item">
    <span class="why-header">
      <svg class="why-icon" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round"><rect x="3" y="11" width="18" height="11" rx="2" ry="2"/><path d="M7 11V7a5 5 0 0 1 10 0v4"/></svg>
      <span class="why-title">Irrevocable Finality</span>
    </span>
    <span class="why-desc">Transactions are final when confirmed. No reorgs, no probabilistic finality.</span>
  </div>
  <div class="why-item">
    <span class="why-header">
      <svg class="why-icon" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round"><path d="M12 22s8-4 8-10V5l-8-3-8 3v7c0 6 8 10 8 10z"/></svg>
      <span class="why-title">Less Ordering Dependence</span>
    </span>
    <span class="why-desc">Commutative deltas produce the same merged state in any order. Conflicting transactions are excluded instead of being serialized inside the block.</span>
  </div>
</div>
